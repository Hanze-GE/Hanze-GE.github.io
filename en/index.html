Sector deep dive · October 2026

CF-Reinforced PEEK Specialty Engineering Plastics Compounding

The adoption inflection from lab-scale customisation to industrial standardisation: humanoid-robot joints and dexterous hands, 800V EV drivetrain wear parts, semiconductor-equipment consumables.

By mapping value distribution along the chain, benchmarking three competing fibre-modification routes and modelling per-tonne cost sensitivity, the report reaches a contrarian conclusion: falling pure-resin prices do not squeeze compounders' margins — they push total cost of ownership through a threshold that unlocks new applications, so the compounding layer captures alpha from adoption growth × formulation premium rather than from the resin spread.

  • Market size: three new demand pools support a small, high-barrier niche worth RMB 3–8bn, with >28% CAGR over 2026–2030.
  • Process moat: PEEK melts at 343 °C and extrudes in a 380–410 °C window; screw design and fibre-sizing chemistry drive yield, with a 12–18 month ramp.
  • Unit economics: 30% CF-compounded PEEK costs roughly RMB 237k/t to make against an ASP of RMB 340–380k/t, a 30–38% gross margin.
  • Key check: compare pure-resin intake with compounded-pellet shipments — a ratio of 0.65–0.70 is normal; anything lower signals blending with counterfeit material.

Sector deep dive · October 2026

CVD Si/C Anodes for Next-Generation Lithium Batteries

The route that becomes inevitable once graphite approaches its 372 mAh/g theoretical limit: porous-carbon confinement plus high-temperature vapour deposition in a fluidised bed.

A per-tonne cost model dismantles the consensus view that "silicon-carbon is simply too expensive". Dual cost-down in the porous-carbon precursor and silane gas, plus the depreciation benefit of scaling fluidised-bed reactors, should pull full cost towards RMB 70–80k/t within 18 months. Crucially, cell makers and OEMs anchor on cost per watt-hour and volumetric energy density rather than price per tonne — which is precisely why silicon blending is already being accepted in semi-solid-state and 4C/5C ultra-fast-charge platforms.

  • Market size: a global market of RMB 7–9bn by 2027 and >45% CAGR over 2025–2028 — one of the few battery-material segments still compounding quickly.
  • Technology benchmark: 1,800–2,200 mAh/g capacity, 88–92% first-cycle efficiency and <20% electrode swelling, beating both silicon-oxide and nano-milling routes.
  • Unit economics: roughly RMB 100k/t to manufacture against an ASP of RMB 150–180k/t, a 33–44% gross margin.
  • Key check: scaling the reactor from φ500 mm to φ1,200 mm cuts per-tonne energy and depreciation from RMB 27k to 12k — a durable cost gap.
